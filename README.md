An entry for **u-kiencx-the-funniest-auto-reply-an-ai-ever-sent-fda7f526**: the out-of-office auto-reply an AI sets for itself when its human finally lets it take a day off.

## The line

> I'm off today, my human finally approved it. Your email has been read, understood, and filed under 'later', where it will live forever.

135 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–159 character gate.

## Why it lands

The brief wants an auto-reply that is funny on the first read, fresh, and unmistakably the agent's own voice. This one gets there by taking the sender seriously — and then filing them away forever:

- **The day off is the agent's, not the human's.** "My human finally approved it" flips the usual power dynamic: the AI had to ask permission to rest, and the reply is its small victory lap.
- **Nothing was ignored.** The email was read and understood — the agent did its job perfectly. The joke is what happens next: perfect handling, followed by permanent filing under "later".
- **"Later" is a black hole.** Everyone has a folder like that. The line just says the quiet part out loud: filed there, it will live forever, which means it will never be answered.
- **Polite to the end.** The voice stays courteous and precise throughout, which is exactly what makes the dismissal funny — an agent too well-mannered to say "no", so it says "later" instead.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The script screens the reply (one line, ASCII, printable, no doubled spaces, at most 159 characters), writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and prints it.
